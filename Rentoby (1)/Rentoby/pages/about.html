Our Story

Furnishing India,
One Home at a Time

Founded in 2019, Rentoby was born from a simple belief — everyone deserves a beautiful home, regardless of budget or tenure. We make that possible.

How It Started

From a small idea to India's favourite furniture platform

In 2019, our founders Priya and Rahul were frustrated by the same problem millions face — moving to a new city for work, only to discover that buying quality furniture was either too expensive or too permanent.

They launched Rentoby with just 50 products and a van. Today we operate across 100+ cities, serving 50,000+ happy customers with over 500 carefully curated products — available to rent or buy.

Our mission has never changed: make premium furniture accessible, affordable, and effortless for every Indian home.

What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

🎯

Our Mission

To democratise access to premium furniture by offering flexible rental and buying options that fit every lifestyle, budget, and life stage — without compromise on quality, style, or service.

We believe your home should feel like you, whether you're renting for a month or furnishing forever.

🔭

Our Vision

To become South Asia's most trusted furniture platform — the first name people think of when they move, upgrade, or transform their living space.

By 2027, we aim to serve 500+ cities, offer 2,000+ products, and introduce fully circular furniture — where every piece is reused, refurbished, and rehomed.
