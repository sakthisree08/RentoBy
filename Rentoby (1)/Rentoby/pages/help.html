Help Center

How can we help you?

Search our knowledge base or browse categories below. We're always here.

Renting Furniture
Renting is simple and takes just a few minutes:
  • Browse our Rent page and choose your furniture.
  • Select your preferred rental period — Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
  • Add to cart and proceed to checkout.
  • Enter your delivery address and pay securely online.
  • We deliver and assemble — usually within 24–48 hours!
No security deposit required. Cancel or return anytime.
Yes! You can extend your rental at any time from your Profile dashboard under "My Rentals". Extensions are billed at the same rate as your current plan. There are no penalties for extending — in fact, longer plans come with better pricing.
Absolutely. Rentoby allows you to swap your rented item for a different product once per rental cycle at no extra charge. Upgrades (switching to a higher-value item) involve a simple top-up payment. Contact our support team to initiate a swap.
Buying Furniture
Buying is straightforward:
  • Visit our Buy page and browse 50+ premium products.
  • Click "Buy Now" or add to cart.
  • Checkout securely with UPI, Card, Net Banking, or EMI.
  • Receive delivery within 2–5 business days, with free assembly included.
All purchases come with a 1-year warranty and 7-day easy return policy.
Yes. All furniture purchased from Rentoby includes a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering manufacturing defects. Premium products (marked with "Top Rated") come with a 2-year warranty. Warranty does not cover damage caused by misuse or accidents — but we offer optional furniture protection plans.
Returns & Refunds
We offer a 7-day hassle-free return policy on all purchases. If you're not completely happy with your order:
  • Initiate a return from your Profile → My Orders within 7 days of delivery.
  • Our team will schedule a free pickup at your convenience.
  • Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method.
For rentals, you can return items at any time — just give us 48 hours' notice and we'll arrange pickup for free.
Once your return is picked up and inspected (usually 1–2 business days), refunds are processed within 5–7 business days. The time to reflect in your account depends on your bank or payment provider — typically 2–3 additional working days for cards, and near-instant for UPI.
Payment Methods
We accept all major payment methods:
  • UPI: PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM
  • Credit/Debit Cards: Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex
  • Net Banking: All major Indian banks
  • EMI: No-cost EMI on orders above ₹5,000 (3, 6, 12 months)
  • Wallets: Paytm, Amazon Pay, MobiKwik
  • Buy Now Pay Later: ZestMoney, LazyPay
All transactions are secured with 256-bit SSL encryption.
No security deposit is required for any rental on Rentoby. This is one of our core promises. You simply pay the rental amount for your chosen period, and that's it. No hidden charges, no lock-in fees, no surprises.
Delivery & Setup
Delivery timelines depend on your city:
  • Metro cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune): Same-day or next-day delivery.
  • Tier-2 cities: 2–3 business days.
  • Other pin codes: 4–7 business days.
All deliveries include free assembly and setup by our trained team. You'll receive real-time tracking via SMS and email.
Yes, 100% free assembly is included with every order — both rentals and purchases. Our professional installation team will arrive with your delivery, assemble everything in your space, and clean up before leaving. You don't need to lift a finger.
Account & Orders
You can track all your orders and active rentals from your Profile page under "My Orders" or "Active Rentals". We also send real-time SMS and email updates at every stage — order confirmed, out for delivery, delivered, and more.
Orders can be cancelled free of charge within 2 hours of placing them. After dispatch, you can still return the item using our 7-day return policy. To cancel, go to Profile → My Orders → Cancel Order, or contact our support team directly.
